DX BasicsFundamental
Q1.What is AWS Direct Connect?
AA service that links two VPCs in the same region
BA private, dedicated network link from your site to AWS
CA content delivery network for caching websites
DAn encrypted IPsec tunnel over the public internet to a VGW
✓ Correct: B. A private, dedicated network link from your site to AWS

How to think about it: Direct Connect = a private, dedicated network link from your network to AWS (no internet).

Why B is correct: Traffic runs over dedicated circuits and the AWS backbone instead of the public internet.

Why the others are wrong:
A) A service that links two VPCs in the same region — That describes VPC peering or Transit Gateway.
C) A content delivery network for caching websites — That describes Amazon CloudFront.
D) An encrypted IPsec tunnel over the public internet to a VGW — That describes Site-to-Site VPN.

Q2.What is a Direct Connect location?
AA CloudFront edge location that caches HTTP content
BYour own on-premises data center or branch office
CAn AWS Availability Zone inside a region's data centers
DA colocation facility that houses AWS DX routers
✓ Correct: D. A colocation facility that houses AWS DX routers

How to think about it: DX location = third-party colocation facility where AWS routers live.

Why D is correct: DX locations are run by data-center providers such as Equinix; AWS places its DX devices there.

Why the others are wrong:
A) A CloudFront edge location that caches HTTP content — Edge locations cache content and don't terminate DX circuits.
B) Your own on-premises data center or branch office — Your site connects to the DX location, usually over a last-mile circuit.
C) An AWS Availability Zone inside a region's data centers — AZs host compute; DX locations are separate facilities.

Q3.What is the cross-connect in a Direct Connect setup?
AA BGP session between two VPCs in one region
BThe fiber linking your router to the AWS router
CThe 802.1Q VLAN tag used on a virtual interface
DA VPN tunnel kept as a backup path into the VPC subnets
✓ Correct: B. The fiber linking your router to the AWS router

How to think about it: Cross-connect = the physical cable between your (or your partner's) router and the AWS router in the DX location.

Why B is correct: The facility provider runs this fiber between the two cages.

Why the others are wrong:
A) A BGP session between two VPCs in one region — VPCs don't run BGP with each other.
C) The 802.1Q VLAN tag used on a virtual interface — The VLAN is a logical layer 2 setting on top of the cable.
D) A VPN tunnel kept as a backup path into the VPC subnets — A backup VPN is a separate path, not the cross-connect.

Q4.Which features must your router support to use Direct Connect?
AIPsec with IKEv2 and pre-shared keys only
B802.1Q VLANs and BGP with MD5 authentication
CSpanning Tree and LACP, without any BGP
DOSPF areas and PIM multicast routing toward AWS
✓ Correct: B. 802.1Q VLANs and BGP with MD5 authentication

How to think about it: DX router needs: single-mode fiber optics, 802.1Q VLANs, BGP with MD5.

Why B is correct: Each VIF is a VLAN, and routes are exchanged over an MD5-authenticated BGP session.

Why the others are wrong:
A) IPsec with IKEv2 and pre-shared keys only — IPsec is needed only if you add a VPN on top of DX.
C) Spanning Tree and LACP, without any BGP — LACP is used for LAGs, but VLANs and BGP are the core requirements.
D) OSPF areas and PIM multicast routing toward AWS — DX uses BGP only; multicast is not carried.

BAuto-negotiation on, with half duplex as the fallback
CAuto-negotiation off, speed and duplex set manually
DJumbo frames of 9001 bytes required on the port
✓ Correct: C. Auto-negotiation off, speed and duplex set manually

Why the others are wrong:

Q6.A team needs a private link to AWS in two weeks, but a new dedicated DX connection is expected to take far longer. What is the best interim plan?
AOrder a LAG instead, because LAGs are provisioned instantly
BEnable SiteLink on the requested connection to speed it up
CRun a Site-to-Site VPN until the DX circuit is ready
DUse VPC peering from the data center to the VPC
✓ Correct: C. Run a Site-to-Site VPN until the DX circuit is ready

How to think about it: DX takes weeks to months to provision, so bridge the gap with a Site-to-Site VPN.

Why C is correct: A VPN can be live in hours and later stay on as a DX backup.

Why the others are wrong:
A) Order a LAG instead, because LAGs are provisioned instantly — A LAG is built from DX connections, which take the same lead time.

D) Use VPC peering from the data center to the VPC — Peering connects VPCs, not on-premises networks.

Q7.What is a dedicated Direct Connect connection?
AA virtual interface created for you by another account
BA slice of a partner's port shared with other customers
CA physical port for one customer, ordered from AWS
DA VPN tunnel that comes with a guaranteed bandwidth SLA
✓ Correct: C. A physical port for one customer, ordered from AWS

How to think about it: Dedicated = a physical port owned by you, ordered from AWS (1, 10, 100 or 400 Gbps).

B) A slice of a partner's port shared with other customers — That is a hosted connection.
D) A VPN tunnel that comes with a guaranteed bandwidth SLA — VPNs run over the internet and have no bandwidth guarantee.

Q8.What is a hosted Direct Connect connection?
AA DX connection that AWS hosts in your own data center
BA partner-provisioned slice of a port, often sub-1G
CA second VIF added to an existing dedicated connection
DA full 100 Gbps port that you order directly from AWS
✓ Correct: B. A partner-provisioned slice of a port, often sub-1G

Why B is correct: Partners offer capacities like 50 Mbps to 10 Gbps and more, carved out of their interconnect.

Why the others are wrong:
A) A DX connection that AWS hosts in your own data center — AWS doesn't put DX routers in customer premises.
C) A second VIF added to an existing dedicated connection — Extra VIFs are not a separate connection type.
D) A full 100 Gbps port that you order directly from AWS — Ordering a full port from AWS is a dedicated connection.

Q11.What is the difference between a hosted connection and a hosted VIF?

✓ Correct: D. Hosted VIF: on another account's connection; hosted connection: a partner circuit

DHosted connections are policed at the purchased speed

Q18.Which MTU values does a transit VIF support?
AOnly 1500 bytes
BUp to 65535 bytes
C1500 or 9001 bytes
D1500 or 8500
✓ Correct: D. 1500 or 8500

How to think about it: MTU: private VIF 1500/9001, transit VIF 1500/8500, public VIF 1500.

B) Up to 65535 bytes — No Ethernet link in AWS supports frames that large.

Q33.What does the minimum links setting on a LAG control?
AThe minimum number of VIFs that must exist on the LAG
BThe minimum BGP hold time for sessions on the LAG
CThe minimum speed allowed for each connection in the LAG
DHow many members must be up for the LAG to stay up
✓ Correct: D. How many members must be up for the LAG to stay up

How to think about it: LAG minimum links = how many members must be up for the LAG to stay up.

Why D is correct: If fewer members are up, the whole LAG goes down so traffic can fail over elsewhere.

Why the others are wrong:
A) The minimum number of VIFs that must exist on the LAG — VIF count is unrelated to this setting.
